How this is worked out

Each policy's commission rate is taken from LIC's own "EstCom" column on the due lists (5% for most renewals, 6% for some plans, 7.5% on 2nd/3rd-year premiums, falling to 5% from year 4). Every policy is rolled forward on its payment mode until its last premium date. Paying book = paid-up policies plus those due since the latest LIC list whose payment LIC hasn't confirmed yet. Overdue and lapsed only earn if the client pays. Star Health: each policy renews once a year at its quoted (or last year's) premium; commission is estimated at 15% until a Star commission statement is added. Commission is before TDS; new policies sold from today are not included.
